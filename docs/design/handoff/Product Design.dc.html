The accent doesn't flip. Amber with dark text clears 7.4:1 on both grounds, so the one colour that means "the app is telling you something" stays identical — which is what makes the two themes feel like one product.
Speaker colours do flip. They have to: at L 0.74 they're legible on black and invisible on white, so light mode drops them to L 0.5 and keeps the same two hues. Same identity, different lightness.
The video plate stays dark in both. The one thing that must never invert — a light surround changes how you judge exposure and skin tone. Light mode only lifts it enough to sit inside a bright window without looking like a hole.
