Cutroom 0.4.0 trims dead air from the audio itself, exports subtitles and an MP3 next to the video, and cuts out to a wide shot when someone has been talking for a long time. It is also the first build where the Windows version can process a recording.

### Tighter edits

- **Dead air is measured from the audio.** Pauses used to be guessed from the gaps between speakers, which meant laughs and murmurs got cut and pauses in the middle of an answer were missed. Now Cutroom listens for real silence. On a 53-minute test episode it removed 1 minute 27 seconds, all of it silence, including 22 pauses inside answers.
- **You can see every cut before you export.** A new Cuts row on the timeline shows each pause it will remove. Click one to keep it. The preview skips the cuts, and the export uses exactly the same list. There are three strengths (pauses over 2s, 1s or 0.5s), and a quarter second of air is kept either side of each cut.
- **Long answers no longer sit on one face.** When someone talks for a long stretch, the edit now cuts out to a wide shot at the end of a sentence or a pause, then back in. On the test episode the longest time on one face dropped from almost 10 minutes to under a minute. Episodes you have already edited keep the shots you chose.
- **Framing styles say what they do.** They are now called Stay wide, Long answers and Most answers, and each one explains its rule. Select a stretch of the transcript to give it its own style, so an introduction can stay wide while an interview cuts in close.

### New exports

- **A subtitle file (.srt)** for YouTube and other players.
- **An MP3 for your podcast feed**, cut from the same edit as the video.
- Fixed: burned-in captions failed when the folder path contained an apostrophe.

### A clearer editor

- **Captions show in the preview** the way they will appear in the export, and "Captions on" is a real switch.
- **The transcript follows along** word by word as the video plays. Click any word to jump to it.
- **Fix a misheard word.** Double-click a word to correct it. If the same word was misheard elsewhere, Cutroom offers to fix every place at once, with an undo.
- **Each person gets one named lane** on the timeline, in their own colour, and you can rename someone at any time.
- **The preview plays more smoothly** and shows exactly the crop the export will use.

### Your work is safer

- **Edits save automatically.** Quit and reopen, and the episode is where you left it.
- **Save an episode as a .cutroom file** to back it up or move it to another computer. It holds everything except the recording itself, so it is about 1 MB rather than gigabytes.
- **Cutroom checks disk space first.** If a recording or export won't fit, it says so before starting, instead of failing partway through.

### Windows

- The Windows version could not process any recording, because of a Windows permission rule. That is fixed. Quitting now closes everything Cutroom started, and no stray console windows appear.
- The README has Windows install steps, including what to do about the SmartScreen warning.

### Also

- **Report a problem** saves Cutroom's recent log to your Downloads folder, with folder names removed, ready to attach to an issue.
- **Optional usage counts.** On first launch Cutroom asks whether to send anonymous counts (such as whether setup finished). They are off unless you say yes, never include your recording, and can be turned off from the title bar. [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/jain-eshan/cutroom/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) lists every event.
- Downloads now come with SHA-256 checksums, listed below, so you can check a file is the real one.
