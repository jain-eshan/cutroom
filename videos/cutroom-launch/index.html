Cutroom
Podcast video that cuts itself.
Step 1 of 4
Drop in your recording.
One camera, two to four people, one mixed audio track. Cutroom writes the transcript and finds each face.
Step 2 of 4
It cuts to whoever's talking.
It matches each voice to a face by lip movement, then frames the shot on them.
Step 3 of 4
Every cut is yours to change.
Pick who's in the shot, drag a cut, or put back a pause it trimmed.
Step 4 of 4
Export the finished video.
MP4 at full resolution, plus a subtitle file and an MP3 for your podcast feed.
